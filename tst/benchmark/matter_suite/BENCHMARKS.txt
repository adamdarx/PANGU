PANGU II matter and black-hole-formation benchmark protocol
Frozen initial protocol: 2026-11-02. Status: implementation and qualification.

Scope approved by the user
  Vacuum: Apples with Apples, isolated punctures, binary punctures.
  Scalar hair: nonlinear EMS without and with scalar self-interaction.
  Formation: scalar collapse and head-on neutron-star collision.
  GRMHD applications: magnetized binary-black-hole accretion and collapse of
  an unstable magnetized star. Formation has two distinct subcases.
  Existing qualified vacuum data are retained; this protocol does not restart
  or cancel existing jobs. New non-core models/initializers belong in plugins.

Primary references and the precise scope of comparison
  EMS-0: Guo, Wang, Zhang, arXiv:2403.02089, sections II.3 and III.1,
    equations (23)-(25), figures 2-3. M=1, Q=0.9, f=exp(alpha*phi^2),
    alpha=5 and 10, V=0; RN seed plus a small neutral scalar perturbation.
    Growth reference for alpha=5: 0.07335. Compare the same observable,
    log(abs(d phi_h/dt)), on the resolved linear growth interval.
    Horizon values read from a plot are illustrative, not raw reference data.
  EMS-V: Fernandes, arXiv:2003.01045, static massive/self-interacting EMS
    solutions and spherical stability. Compare action-normalized static ODEs,
    not a visually similar curve or an unconverted quartic coefficient.
    Our action is [R-2(dphi)^2-4V-f F^2]/(16*pi), and
    V=mu^2*phi^2/2+lambda*phi^4/2+nu*phi^6/6. Initial series: alpha=5,
    lambda=0 and 10, mu=nu=0. This series is our benchmark extension;
    it is not claimed to reproduce a particular Fernandes figure.
  SC: Choptuik, PRL 70, 9 (1993), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.70.9;
    Gundlach, Price, Pullin, arXiv:gr-qc/9307010. Neutral massless scalar
    collapse from a regular, initially untrapped configuration. Initially
    qualify dispersion and black-hole formation, not critical exponents.
  NS-HO: Miller, Suen, Tobias, arXiv:gr-qc/9904041, PRD 63, 121501.
    Head-on neutron-star collision can promptly collapse for the specified
    EOS and masses. Their specified case has two 1.4 solar-mass stars,
    Gamma=2, initial K=1.16e5 cm^5/(g*s^2), initial proper separation 44 km,
    infall speed sqrt(G*M_star/d), and a York constraint solve after boosted
    TOV superposition. Preserve shock heating: K is not fixed during evolution.
    A start-at-rest collision is a different case, not this exact benchmark.
    Do not substitute a black-hole--neutron-star collision benchmark.
  BBH-MHD: Kelly et al., arXiv:1710.02132, homogeneous magnetized plasma
    surrounding an equal-mass binary, orbital-axis-aligned field. Reference
    the full text for EOS, density normalization, magnetization and duration.
    IMPORTANT: this paper neglects plasma stress-energy in Einstein's equations.
    Its rho0=1 and b0=0.1 use an independently rescalable fluid normalization;
    feeding rho0=1 into the full Einstein source is not the same experiment.
    Canonical Table 1: d=14.384 M, puncture mass=0.4902240,
    |P_tang|=0.07563734 M, inward |P_rad|=0.0002963 M, Gamma=4/3,
    p0/rho0=0.2, specific internal energy=0.6, b0^2/rho0=0.01.
    Reference merger time is approximately 3514.333 M. A shorter separation
    pilot must have its own documented initial data and cannot be claimed to
    reproduce that particular merger time. Paper's Poynting proxy and direct
    radial electromagnetic energy flux are distinct diagnostics.
    A circumbinary torus is a different initial-data family: see Farris et al.,
    arXiv:1207.3354. It must not be silently substituted for the cloud.
  NS-C: Duez, Shapiro, Yo, arXiv:gr-qc/0401076, and Cook et al.,
    arXiv:2311.04989, establish collapse-to-BH and post-formation evolution
    tests. Cook et al.'s D4 model is rotating and uses RNS; a nonrotating
    unstable TOV model is explicitly a different benchmark. Qualify the
    nonmagnetic limit before adding a specified weak field and measuring
    the magnetic contribution to the initial constraint error.
  TOV control: AsterX, arXiv:2406.11669, section 4.7, Gamma=2, K=100,
    rho_c=1.28e-3. A field added to an unmagnetized TOV profile is a
    perturbation, not an exact magnetohydrostatic equilibrium.
    Independent TOV integration gives M=1.40015973 and areal R=9.58562 for
    rho_c=1.28e-3. The admission unstable model rho_c=7.993e-3 gives
    M=1.44759419, R=5.83811, on the descending branch beyond the maximum
    near rho_c=3.2e-3. Admission uses v_pert=-0.01 and b_norm=1.
    A short finite run of this model is not yet evidence of BH formation.

Acceptance common to all cases
  A scheduler exit code or visual appearance alone is not acceptance.
  Record source/submodule commit, uncommitted diff if any, executable SHA256,
    actual compiler flags, input, execution command, hardware, rank binding,
    wall time, cell updates, final physical time and every job identifier.
  Three physical resolutions, one run per resolution. Record spacings and
    resolved physical scales, not only root-grid N. Do not demand fourth
    order at stellar surfaces/shocks; report the measured convergence order.
  Require finite evolved fields, valid conserved-to-primitive recovery,
    documented floor/fallback counts and diagnostic masks, and valid horizon
    residuals. Missing or failed diagnostics remain explicit failures/missing
    values; never fill them by carrying forward the last successful value.
  Perform a restart check and a representative boundary/CFL control before
    declaring the corresponding family accepted. These are numerical controls,
    not additional physical parameter surveys.
  Fix acceptance tolerances before inspecting the production comparison.
    Numerical tolerances below are project targets, not universal standards.

EMS production series (existing 8192-cell pilot can be retained with provenance)
  One radial block/rank, PG constrained metric, classical RK4, CFL=0.3,
    KO=0.05, nonlinear constraint iteration tolerance=1e-11.
  r in [1.1,600], T=1000; N=8192,16384,32768. Report actual h. The scalar
    seed amplitude is 1e-6, center=10, width=3, P=0. Preserve the original
    alpha=5 pilot's 12 iterations; use 12 for new production runs.
  Compare phi and N on the common exterior r in [1.05*r_h,30], using
    interpolation sufficiently accurate to resolve the measured order.
  Require: charge relative/absolute residual <1e-12; all nonlinear solves
    converge; exterior constraint and inter-resolution differences decrease;
    alpha=5 linear growth agrees with 0.07335 within 1%; highest-resolution
    exterior phi maximum difference from an independent static ODE <1e-4.
  The static ODE seeded by measured (r_h,phi_h) checks the remaining exterior
    equations. It is not an independent prediction of those boundary values.
  Independently budget finite-radius mass plus its time-integrated flux.
    Refine history quadrature and compare the residual with its discretization
    uncertainty. An ADM estimate with only the Coulomb tail excludes scalar
    energy outside the computational domain.
  A larger-radius run at the same h checks boundary sensitivity. Radius and
    extraction windows must also satisfy the characteristic causal estimate.
  Scalar-hair perturbation runs require a constraint-consistent profile loader
    and a documented perturbation. No claim of arbitrary-perturbation or
    nonspherical stability follows from a single spherical perturbation.

Formation / 3D matter admission
  SC: regular center, initially no trapped surfaces, independent horizon
    condition, two pulse amplitudes giving dispersion and collapse; then
    convergence of the BH mass and energy budget. Excision may start only
    inside a detected trapped region and must remain causally outflowing.
  NS-HO: constraint-qualified binary fluid initial data, shock-capable thermal
    EOS, common horizon, remnant mass, GW signal and exterior matter budget.
  BBH-MHD: magnetic divergence control, single-BH and zero-field limits,
    qualified binary geometry, matter initialization and stress-energy coupling;
    horizon accretion rates and EM fluxes at specified extraction radii. A
    weak-plasma benchmark alone does not establish measurable orbital feedback.
  NS-C: demonstrate the selected star's unstable branch and controlled inward
    perturbation; verify AH formation and post-horizon evolution, exterior
    constraints, mass/flux budget and magnetic divergence.
  Before large 3D production: measure memory and time per step in a short run,
    select h and AMR extents from the stellar/horizon scales, then choose GPU
    count from measured memory. Use existing validated CUDA-aware MPI launch
    and keep HOST_COMM_BUFFERS=OFF. Default QoS=low; no added wall-time limit.

State transitions
  planned -> literature_locked -> implemented -> smoke_passed -> formal_running
    -> completed_pending_analysis -> accepted (or rejected_with_evidence).
  Remaining unimplemented initial-data families are not submitted as production
    jobs merely because an input file exists. Each family's diagnostics and
    limitations must be included in the final error/performance table.
